es.lex - Spanish lexicon of the grammar engine

Source: the Spanish dictionary of Wiktionary, as extracted by Tatu Ylonen's wiktextract and published on kaikki.org
(https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Spanish/). The text of Wiktionary is available under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license (CC BY-SA 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ ; the contributors
are listed in the history of each page of en.wiktionary.org.

What is kept: for every one-word entry, its lemma, part of speech and inflected forms (gender, number, person, mood and tense).
The text of the definitions is not kept. The file is made by tools/build_lexicon_es.py; it is a derived work and is shared under
the same license, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Format: the one of fr.lex (see grammar_engine/lexicon.py). The inflected forms of a participle (comprados) have the participle
(comprado) as lemma and no verb form.
