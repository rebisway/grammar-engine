en.lex - English lexicon of the grammar engine

Source: the English dictionary of Wiktionary, as extracted by Tatu Ylonen's wiktextract and published on kaikki.org
(https://kaikki.org/dictionary/English/). The text of Wiktionary is available under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license (CC BY-SA 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ ; the contributors
are listed in the history of each page of en.wiktionary.org.

What is kept: for every one-word entry, its lemma, part of speech and inflected forms (number, person, tense,
degree of an adjective or an adverb). The text of the definitions is not kept. The file is made by tools/build_lexicon_en.py;
it is a derived work and is shared under the same license, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Format: the one of fr.lex (see grammar_engine/lexicon.py). For an adjective or an adverb the gender field holds the
degree: 1 comparative, 2 superlative.
