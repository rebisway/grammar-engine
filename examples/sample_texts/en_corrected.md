## Subject and verb

He goes to school every day. They go home early. I am happy today. We were late again. He doesn't know the answer. The dogs run in the park. Everyone is ready. There are many people outside. He can go home now.

## Determiners and nouns

A book is on the table. These books are old. She ate an apple. He is a dog lover. This information is useful.

## Verb forms

I have gone home. She has eaten lunch already. I saw him yesterday. If I had known, I would have come. I have read the book twice.

## Spelling of look-alike words

It is better than nothing. The box is too big for the shelf. You will lose the game. I don't know what to say. You should have called me. It's a beautiful day.

## Capitals and repetitions

Yesterday I went to the market. The cat sat on the mat. It rained all day. The streets were empty.
