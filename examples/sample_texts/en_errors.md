# Sample text: mistakes in English

## Subject and verb

He go to school every day. They goes home early. I is happy today. We was late again. He don't know the answer. The dogs runs in the park. Everyone are ready. There is many people outside. He can go home now.

## Determiners and nouns

A books are on the table. These book is old. She ate a apple. He is an dog lover. This information is useful.

## Verb forms

I have went home. She has ate lunch already. I seen him yesterday. If I would have known, I would have come. I have read the book twice.

## Spelling of look-alike words

It is better then nothing. The box is to big for the shelf. You will loose the game. I dont know what to say. You should of called me. Its a beautiful day.

## Capitals and repetitions

Yesterday i went to the market. The cat sat on the the mat. It rained all day. the streets were empty.
